# Figure 5: epithelial program replication and spatial support

Date: 2026-09-16. This is an exploratory extension. GSE310797 and existing C1/M1/M5 associations have already been inspected. This document fixes the new rules before computing the new endpoints; it is not a prospective preregistration. No survival outcomes or models are used.

## Discovery and signature audit

Use the original 5,609 GSE181919 epithelial-compartment cells and C0-C8 labels. Exclude mixed C5 and immune-like C8 from the C1-versus-other comparison. Use log1p(CP10,000) over the 16,097 genes in the shared count object. Summarize C1-minus-other expression and detection differences separately for each discovery patient with at least 10 C1 and 10 other eligible cells. Only five patients meet this pairing threshold (P6, P15, P21, P31, P51); all counts and excluded patients are reported. This limits signature generalizability and is not hidden by the total cell count.

Retain genes with patient-mean C1 detection >=20%, positive expression difference in >=70% of eligible patients, positive mean expression and detection differences, excluding MT-, RPL, RPS, IGH/IGK/IGL and TRAV/TRBV technical/receptor prefixes. Rank by patient-mean expression difference times positive patient-mean detection difference, then gene name. Select up to 60 genes; do not force unavailable genes into the set. This is descriptive patient-balanced marker selection, not a list of 60 statistically significant DEGs. Record leave-one-patient-out direction consistency and rankings as discovery sensitivity without using them to retune the list.

Freeze alternating ranked genes as C1_score (odd ranks) and C1_check (even ranks). Check genes do not contribute to C1_score but are selected in the same discovery cohort; they are not independent discoveries. Full C1 set, both halves, modules, control sets, software and source hashes are exported before external scoring. Modules M1/M5 retain original formal hdWGCNA membership; remove the entire C1 set from module scores for primary association analyses. Use fixed basal controls KRT5/KRT14/KRT15/COL17A1/ITGA6 and proliferation controls MKI67/TOP2A/PCNA/MCM2/MCM4/TYMS; remove these control genes from the C1 scoring/checking halves to avoid direct overlap with covariates. Export the removals. No four-gene list or old bulk-selected genes is imposed.

## Independent-cohort program support

Use every previously annotated GSE310797 epithelial cell (24,000; 16 patients) without filtering patients by results. Use continuous scores, not per-patient high-score quantiles or transferred cluster labels. Normalize separately within cells; no joint embedding is used to establish replication. Mean expression and detection coverage are reported for every signature. UMAP is not required for the primary evidence.

Compute within-patient partial Spearman associations, adjusting rank-transformed library size, detected genes and mitochondrial percentage. The C1_score versus C1_check endpoint is also evaluated after adjustment for disjoint basal/proliferation controls. Retain both estimates to distinguish technical from biological adjustment. Show check-gene associations as a descriptive heatmap (no gene-level significance claims). Use 100 discovery-expression/detection-matched random checking sets as a specificity baseline, report empirical exceedance without treating it as proof of cancer specificity. Matching uses discovery quantile bins only and excludes the frozen C1 and control genes. No external associations are used for selection.

Primary donor-direction sign tests: C1 score/check (QC-adjusted), C1 score/check (QC + basal/proliferation), C1/M1-disjoint and C1/M5-disjoint (QC-adjusted), corrected jointly across four tests. Report patient-median rho, patient-bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000 draws), all patient effects, and a sensitivity excluding patients with <100 epithelial cells. The continuous C1 score is also summarized across discovery clusters and tissue labels; this is descriptive and not independent validation. A repeated basal program is not proof of a malignant subtype, stemness, lineage transition, or full network preservation.

## Orthogonal spatial support

Use all 23,029 retained GSE208253 spots, 12 sections/10 patients, and existing fixed Fig2 niche assignments. Normalize raw spatial counts within spot over the available SC/spatial shared gene universe. Display actual signature coverage; spatial scores are not placed on a quantitatively equivalent scale to single-cell scores. Use observed mixed-spot expression; do not label it cell-type-specific expression. Score fixed C1_score, C1_check and disjoint M1/M5. Display representative S6 and S2, selected previously by Fig2 cell composition (not by new program results), with common per-program color ranges. Include maps for all sections in a companion PDF.

Primary spatial comparisons: N2 immune-mixed versus N1 fibrovascular, and N3 epithelial-rich versus N1, for C1_score, M1-disjoint and M5-disjoint. For each section/contrast, restrict epithelial abundance to the overlap of the two groups' 10th-90th percentile ranges. Require >=30 retained spots per group; record failures and support ranges. Regress the section-standardized program score on niche, a 4-df spline of log1p(cell2location epithelial abundance), log total counts, log detected genes and mitochondrial percentage. Use standardized nuisance variables; report matrix rank/conditioning and no spot-level P values. Summarize repeated sections within patient with equal section weights, then give each patient equal weight. Report median standardized niche coefficient, 10,000 patient-bootstrap interval, exact patient sign test with BH over six comparisons; fewer than six eligible patients is insufficient for a primary inferential claim. Spatial dependence is preserved within patients in resampling; no independent-spot inference is used. Sensitivity adds a quadratic x/y spatial trend within section and separately replaces epithelial abundance with its proportion. No stage-difference claim is a primary endpoint.

Niches and abundance are derived from the same deconvolution. Residual association is conditional mixed-spot spatial support, not proof of epithelial-intrinsic expression, causal niche effects, or independent validation of Fig2.
